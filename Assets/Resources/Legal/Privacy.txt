PRIVACY POLICY - ABYSSAL FISH

Last updated: [10/23/2026]
Made by: [ABYSSAL TEAM]
Contact: [EMAIL]


SHORT VERSION

We collect nothing. Nothing you do in this game is sent anywhere. The game has
no internet connection, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The only thing it
saves is your game progress, and it stays on your phone.


1. WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing.

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. We never
see any information about you or your device. We could not identify you if we
wanted to.

Specifically, we do not collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number or any contact details
- Your location
- Your device ID, advertising ID or any other identifier
- Your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera
- Your IP address
- Any usage statistics, crash reports or analytics
- Anything else


2. WHAT IS SAVED ON YOUR PHONE

The game saves your progress on your own device, using the standard app storage
the operating system provides. It is five numbers:

1. Your best distance (your high score)
2. How many coins you have
3. Which fish you have unlocked
4. Which fish you are currently using
5. Whether sound is on or off

That is the complete list. These stay on your device. They are never uploaded,
never shared, and never seen by us or by anyone else.


3. NO INTERNET CONNECTION

The game does not connect to the internet. It has no servers, no backend and no
online features.

- There are no accounts and no logins.
- There are no advertisements.
- There are no analytics or tracking tools.
- There are no third-party services, SDKs or trackers of any kind.
- There are no in-app purchases. The coins in the game are not real money,
  cannot be bought, and have no value outside the game.


4. PERMISSIONS

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera,
microphone, photos or files.


5. HOW TO DELETE YOUR DATA

Because everything is stored only on your device, you control all of it:

- Uninstall the app. Everything is deleted with it.
- Or, to keep the app but reset your progress: Settings > Apps > Abyssal Fish >
  Storage > Clear data.

You do not need to ask us, and there is nothing for us to delete on our side.


6. CHILDREN

We do not collect data from anyone, so we do not collect data from children.

The game has no chat, no social features, no user-generated content, no
advertising and no purchases.


7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If a future version of the game ever collects anything, this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.

The version included in the app you have installed is the version that applies
to you.
